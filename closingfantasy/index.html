Build notes

What changed in each build. Newest first.
v0.23 · Your tuning as defaults
  • Your latest test settings are now the built-in defaults (all 193 values from your export). Settings added after that export keep their current defaults: trail cut on guard, dull block, blocked knockback and push-back, smear on/off per swing.
  • Widened sliders you had pushed to the end: heavy windup hold (to 0.95), glint size (to 80), sword bounce (to 4), dodge slow-mo speed (down to ×0.01), afterimage lifetime (to 2500 ms).
v0.22 · One slash on heavy
  • Heavy swings show one slash: the smear frame is off for heavies, so only the sword trail plays. Before, the smear (big fan from your hand) and the trail (arc around the target) stacked.
  • New switches under ✨ New juice: Smear frame on light swings and on heavy swings.
v0.21 · Blocked push-back
  • A blocked hit pushes you back 20 px for real (your actual position moves, sliding over 120 ms), then you walk back in as usual.
  • New sliders under ⚠ Gameplay: Blocked: push you back (0 = off) and push-back slide time.
v0.20 · Blocked hits feel bad
  • Armor chips moved to heavy hits (6 per heavy). Blocked hits no longer throw chips, so they no longer look like progress.
  • Blocked hits now feel negative: you are knocked back 10 px and tilt away, your arm jolts, the burst and sparks turn dull grey instead of gold, and the GUARDED label shakes.
  • Dull block sound: a flat low thud and a dead clank instead of the bright ring.
  • New sliders under ✨ More juice: dull look and sound on/off, knockback, tilt and arm jolt. New Isolate layer: Blocked: your knockback & arm jolt.
v0.19 · Guard cuts the trail
  • On a guarded hit, your sword trail stops at the shield instead of sweeping through, with a bright white edge where it was cut off.
  • New sliders under ✨ More juice: on/off and where along the arc the cut happens. New Isolate layer: Trail cut off on guard.
v0.18 · Bigger zoom range
  • Zoom punch sliders widened: per-outcome zoom and kill zoom now go up to 60% (was 20%), and zoom duration up to 3000 ms (was 1000 ms). Default values are unchanged.
v0.17 · Defender guards through windup
  • The Defender keeps his guard up during his windup. His loop is now: idle (guarding) → windup (guarding) → swing (guard drops) → 0.7 s cooldown (open) → back to idle.
  • The only openings are right after his swing and after you parry him. Heavy attacks still go through his guard.
v0.16 · Defender starts guarded
  • The Defender opens every fight with his guard up for the full guard time (1.5 s) before his first swing. Before, he started his first windup 0.6–1.1 s in, like every other enemy.
  • His shield sits slightly higher while guarding, so up and down are easy to tell apart.
v0.15 · Defender guard fixed
  • The Defender now guards all the time except during his windup and a 0.7 s opening after his swing (or after you parry him). Then his guard goes back up for 1.5 s before his next windup, and the loop repeats. Heavy attacks still go through his guard.
  • Before, he only guarded while idle, which almost never happened in a fight (2 of 16 light hits).
  • His shield drops while he is open, so you can see the window.
  • Light hits that land during his opening keep him open until it ends.
  • Before, he also started his next windup the instant his cooldown ended, so he never had time to guard.
  • New sliders under ⚠ Gameplay: Defender opening after his swing and Defender guard time between swings. New Isolate layer: Defender shield lowers when open.
v0.14 · Defender guard restored
  • The Defender blocks light attacks again, with the v0.12 guard effects: armor chips, your sword bouncing back, and stronger hitstop, shake and sparks. The guard sound itself is unchanged from v0.4. The player has no guard; only parry and dodge.
  • Fix: v0.13 had switched the Defender's guard off by mistake.
v0.13 · Parry & dodge
  • Parry stance: pressing parry raises your sword into a block pose, with a cyan shimmer arc that fades as the window closes.
  • Parry clash: a white and cyan star flash at the meeting point between you and the attacker, plus 0.35 s slow-motion, a short impact frame (40 ms), a riposte flick of your sword, and a ringing metallic tail on the sound.
  • Stagger stars spin over a parried enemy for as long as it is staggered.
  • Enemy attack swing: every enemy attack now shows a swing arc (or an arrow streak for the Archer), so you can see what you parried or dodged.
  • Dodge slide: you slide to the new spot over 110 ms instead of teleporting (visual only), leaning into it, with 4 afterimages, dust kicked up and a sweeping whoosh.
  • Dodge success: if an attack you were in the path of lands within 0.45 s after you dodged, you get "DODGE", a ring, a chime, a buzz and 0.25 s slow-motion.
  • New ⚔ Parry & dodge group in the Tuning panel and 9 new layers in Isolate effects.
v0.12 · More juice
  • Guarded hits pass: hitstop 45→80 ms, shake 2.5→6, recoil 5→8, burst 16.5→30, sparks 12→24 (faster).
  • Armor chips: metal and wood shards fly off the Defender's shield on guard (8) and on parry (4), bouncing on the ground.
  • Sword bounce: on a guarded hit your sword snaps back instead of following through.
  • Heavy windup hold & glint: the heavy reaches full draw early, holds, and a star glints on the blade tip. Contact timing is unchanged.
  • Squash & stretch: victims squash and spring back on impact; you stretch slightly on the lunge.
  • Dust & scuff marks: dust puffs at the victim's feet and slide marks on the ground in the recoil direction.
  • Zoom punch: a quick zoom toward the contact point on heavy (4%) and parry (2%), and 6% on kills. Holds during hitstop, then eases out.
  • New per-outcome rows (squash, dust, chips, zoom), a ✨ More juice group, and 6 new layers in Isolate effects.
v0.11 · Battle exit & map defeat
  • Your swing finishes before the battle ends: the follow-through plays out, then a 0.25 s settle, then back to the map. Before, your pose froze mid-swing.
  • Map defeat flash: a defeated enemy's map piece blinks white, swells, then bursts into a ring and sparks with a fading "Defeated" (0.8 s).
  • New sliders under Defeat & encounter clear: settle time and map flash duration. New Map defeat flash layer in Isolate effects.
v0.10 · Kill slow-mo
  • Kill slow-motion: time drops to ×0.3 for 0.7 s when an enemy is defeated, then eases back to normal.
  • Impact frames are now kill-only by default (heavy and parry set to 0); your values are built in: 120 ms, strength 0.85, contrast 6.5.
  • New sliders in ✨ New juice: duration, speed, and an "only on last enemy" switch.
  • New Kill slow-motion layer in Isolate effects.
  • Victim shiver off by default (all outcomes 0). Sliders kept for a future earthquake-style attack.
v0.9 · Softer impact frame
  • Impact frame is softer: blended at 40% over the scene instead of 100%, with contrast 5 instead of 14.
  • It now fades out over its last 20 ms instead of cutting off.
  • New sliders in ✨ New juice: Impact frame strength and Impact frame contrast.
v0.8 · Isolate effects
  • Isolate effects panel (top left): solo (S) or mute (M) any of 20 effect layers.
  • Quick buttons: All on, Only v0.6 new, v0.6 new off.
  • Slow-mo (O): ×0.5 / ×0.25 / ×0.1 to see each layer clearly.
  • Status bar shows ISOLATING / SLOW-MO when active. Isolation is never saved.
v0.7 · Build notes panel
  • This panel: a changelog of every build.
  • No gameplay or feel values changed.
v0.6 · Juice pass
  • Victim shiver: the enemy trembles during hitstop while you hold still.
  • Alternating light swings: every second light hit in a chain is a backhand, and the trail mirrors.
  • Smear frame: a stretched blade fills the swing arc on contact.
  • Impact frame: a black-and-white flash on heavy hits, parries and kills.
  • HP damage chunk: lost HP shows white, holds, then drains.
  • Phone vibration on hits, parries and kills (Android only).
  • Your tuning JSON is built in as the defaults; heavy-hit slider ranges widened.
  • Fix: Save as my default now stores every value.
  • New settings: the ✨ New juice group and per-outcome rows in the Tuning panel.

CLOSING FANTASY v0.23 · Your tuning as defaults

Training grounds · 4 enemies

Playtest settings

PC keyboard or mouse. Map: click an enemy to approach and fight. Defeat all four enemies. Battles: move automatically; attack manually. Time stops while planning. Red telegraphs mark committed strikes.
Hit feedback pass: directional recoil, contact bursts, tapered weapon trails, hitstop, falling enemies and layered synthesized audio.
