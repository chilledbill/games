    How to play
    LEFT PADMove by dragging anywhere in the lower-left area; the stick follows your thumb
    FIRELeft click or tap: PISTOLS rapid fire / SHOTGUN heavy knockback. Weapon select: 1 / 2 or top HUD.
    LAUNCHRight click (or Q) launches the target; pistol shots then maintain a short aerial combo.
    BLASTE / BLAST: strong directional knockback; shoot into another foe or wall.
    DODGEEvade; last-second dodges against melee attacks empower your next STRIKE.
    AIR SLAMPC F: slam a launched foe down; nearby enemies take shockwave damage. Ends the juggle.
    COLLISIONBLAST (E) sends foes flying. Line them up to hit another enemy for bonus damage and relaunch.

    AUTO: game picks your target. DIR: move the left stick toward the opponent you want to hit. HYBRID: retains a target; flick the STRIKE button toward another enemy to switch. Tapping an enemy also works in hybrid.

    FEEL LAB checks off successful strikes, air follow-ups, blast hits, and slider comparisons. Tap its header to open the objectives. Switch to the previous targeting tests using the TARGETING tab.

    PASSIVE keeps two targets still for reliable hit-feedback tests; SPARRING activates their movement and attacks. Change the number of foes in pause settings.

    PC HOVER: Move the mouse over an enemy to select it. Moving into empty space retains that target; moving over a different enemy switches. Each attack commits to its target at startup. Out-of-reach targets stay marked but cannot be hit.
