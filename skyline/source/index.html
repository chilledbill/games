01 / OPEN DOJODUMMY / READY
Combat practice

Your opponent uses ground combos, launch/chase, air combos, dodges, counters and extended air routes. Hold B to block attacks from the front. Guard drains on impact; Charged Cleave breaks it faster. Release to recover guard. Dodge stays immediate. Red warning: a strike is coming. Dodge as it connects, then Attack to counter. Break armor with repeated hits or Charged Cleave. Reset restarts the round and clears the checklist.

Left thumb: move, or hold ↑ to launch.
Right thumb: tap ATTACK, then JUMP to chase.
Tap ATTACK in the air. ↑ + Attack relaunches; ↓ + Attack dives.
Dodge then Attack for a dash strike.
Jump after an air hit for Enemy Step (once per launch).
Hold Attack, then release to charge.
