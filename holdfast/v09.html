Holdfast

Stand your ground against three enemies. Tap a threat to turn toward it.

TARGET: Tap any living enemy to face it. Your fighter stays in the center. Attacks already swinging finish on their original target.
LIGHT: Tap for three-hit combos; switch targets between hits.
HEAVY: Hold at least 0.30s and release. Break shields or punish broken posture.
GUARD: Tap to parry or hold to block. Only protects against your selected enemy. Turn toward the next warning before it hits.
THREATS: Red/gold rings and countdowns identify attacks. Only one enemy attacks at a time. Windups last at least 1.5s, followed by 1.4s of breathing room after the attack resolves or is interrupted. Clear all three to begin the next wave.

No upgrades, skills, dodge or stamina. Defeat enemies to recover 10 HP. You can finish earlier.
