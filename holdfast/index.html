Mobile combat prototype

Holdfast

Three buttons. Automatic approach. Light combos and charged heavy strikes.

LIGHT: Tap three times for a fast combo. The third strike is the finisher and has recovery.
HEAVY: Hold for at least 0.30s, then release. Green charge zone gives a perfect heavy. A tap does nothing. Heavy can end a light chain early and has its own recovery.
GUARD: Tap to parry or hold to block. Guard can cancel a light chain or charging.
POSTURE: Hits and parries fill the enemy’s gold meter. Break it to create a 2.2s opening. Land a charged heavy during the opening for a crushing +25 damage punish. Posture fades if you stop applying pressure.
Red flash: enemy attack is about to land. Gold attacks break normal block.

No upgrades, skills, dodge or stamina. Defeat enemies to recover 10 HP. You can finish earlier.
